# Answer Key - Scholarship Practice Year 5 (AAS-style)

Practice only - not an official AAS paper. All original content.

## 1. Abstract Reasoning and Problem Solving (28)

**Q1.** B - hive
- A bird lives in a nest; a bee lives in a hive. The relationship is animal → dwelling.
- Skill: Verbal analogy

**Q2.** C - Circle
- Triangle, square and rectangle are all polygons (straight sides). A circle is curved.
- Skill: Verbal classification

**Q3.** B - quiet
- Hot and cold are opposites; loud and quiet are opposites.
- Skill: Verbal analogy

**Q4.** B - Some Blips might be Quips
- All Blips are inside Zogs. Some Zogs are Quips, so some Blips could overlap with Quips — but it is not certain. The only safe statement is that some Blips might be Quips.
- Skill: Logical reasoning

**Q5.** C - song
- An author creates a book; a composer creates a song (or piece of music).
- Skill: Verbal analogy

**Q6.** A - DUOLC
- Each word is reversed letter-by-letter. CLOUD reversed is DUOLC.
- Skill: Verbal pattern

**Q7.** C - Bat
- Sparrow, eagle and owl are birds. A bat is a mammal.
- Skill: Verbal classification

**Q8.** A - EFTL
- Each letter shifts forward by 1 in the alphabet: L→M, A→B, M→N, P→Q. So D→E, E→F, S→T, K→L → EFTL.
- Skill: Letter coding

**Q9.** B - kerb
- A bank borders a river; a kerb (curb) borders a road.
- Skill: Verbal analogy

**Q10.** C - Carrot
- Orange, banana and grape are fruits. Carrot is a vegetable (root).
- Skill: Verbal classification

**Q11.** B - 48
- Each number is doubled: 3×2=6, 6×2=12, 12×2=24, 24×2=48.
- Skill: Number pattern

**Q12.** C - 30
- Differences increase by 1 each time: +3, +4, +5, +6, then +7 → 23+7=30.
- Skill: Number pattern

**Q13.** B - 26
- Add consecutive odd numbers starting from 3: +3, +5, +7, +9, +11. So 17+9=26, then 26+11=37.
- Skill: Number pattern

**Q14.** B - 81
- Each number maps to its square: 4²=16, 5²=25, 7²=49, 9²=81.
- Skill: Number relationship

**Q15.** C - 13
- Fibonacci sequence: each term is the sum of the two before it. 5+8=13.
- Skill: Number pattern

**Q16.** B - 64
- Subtract 9 each time: 100−9=91, 91−9=82, 82−9=73, 73−9=64.
- Skill: Number pattern

**Q17.** B - 11
- 3 + 3 + 5 = 11.
- Skill: Symbolic reasoning

**Q18.** C - 48
- 16=4², 25=5², 36=6², 49=7². 48 is not a perfect square.
- Skill: Number classification

**Q19.** B - 7
- Work backwards: 17 − 3 = 14, then 14 ÷ 2 = 7.
- Skill: Functional reasoning

**Q20.** A - ● filled circle
- The pattern alternates filled circle, empty square. Next is a filled circle.
- Skill: Figural sequence

**Q21.** B - 2
- One cut through a single fold creates two mirrored holes when unfolded.
- Skill: Spatial reasoning

**Q22.** A - L rotated 90° clockwise (⌐ shape)
- A rotation keeps the same chirality. A left-right flip is a reflection, not a rotation.
- Skill: Spatial rotation

**Q23.** C - 6
- There are 4 small triangles (two on top half split? Actually: the mid horizontal and vertical create: 2 small on top of midline left/right of vertical, 2 below — that is 4 small. Plus 2 medium (left half and right half of the big triangle) = 6. Count carefully: left-top small, right-top small, left-bottom small, right-bottom small, left large half, right large half = 6.
- Skill: Figural counting

**Q24.** A - ○○○
- Alternating filled and empty circles; the group size grows by 1 each time. After three filled comes three empty.
- Skill: Figural sequence

**Q25.** C - 6
- A cube has 6 faces.
- Skill: Spatial knowledge

**Q26.** B - A cross: one centre square with one square on each of four sides, plus one extra on one arm
- A common valid cube net is a cross (plus one extra square on an arm) totaling 6 squares. A row of 6 cannot fold without overlap; 9 squares is too many.
- Skill: Spatial nets

**Q27.** B - 2 pairs
- A 2×2 grid has two pairs of opposite (diagonal) corners.
- Skill: Spatial structure

**Q28.** A - ▼
- The repeating unit is ▲ ▼ ▲▼. After the second ▲, next is ▼.
- Skill: Figural sequence

## 2. Mathematics Achievement and Reasoning (28)

**Q1.** B - 5 241
- 3 482 + 1 759: 482+759=1 241 (carry 1 thousand), 3+1+1=5 → 5 241.
- Skill: Addition

**Q2.** A - 3 522
- 6 000 − 2 478 = 3 522.
- Skill: Subtraction

**Q3.** B - 282
- 40×6=240, 7×6=42, total 282.
- Skill: Multiplication

**Q4.** C - 12
- 12 × 12 = 144, so 144 ÷ 12 = 12.
- Skill: Division

**Q5.** B - 4/6
- Multiply numerator and denominator by 2: 2/3 = 4/6.
- Skill: Fractions

**Q6.** B - 5/8
- Same denominator: 3+2=5, so 5/8.
- Skill: Fractions

**Q7.** A - 7/10
- 0.7 means 7 tenths = 7/10.
- Skill: Decimals

**Q8.** B - 0.4, 0.405, 0.45
- 0.400 < 0.405 < 0.450.
- Skill: Decimals

**Q9.** B - 20
- 25% = 1/4. One quarter of 80 is 20.
- Skill: Percentages

**Q10.** B - 26 cm
- Perimeter = 2×(length + width) = 2×(8+5) = 2×13 = 26 cm.
- Skill: Perimeter

**Q11.** C - 36 m²
- Area = length × width = 9 × 4 = 36 m².
- Skill: Area

**Q12.** C - 155
- 2×60 = 120, plus 35 = 155 minutes.
- Skill: Time

**Q13.** B - 5:25 pm
- 3:45 + 1 hour = 4:45; +40 minutes = 5:25 pm.
- Skill: Time

**Q14.** B - 4 700
- The tens digit is 7 (≥5), so round up the hundreds: 4 700.
- Skill: Rounding

**Q15.** C - 24
- 24 ÷ 4 = 6 and 24 ÷ 6 = 4. Others fail one of the tests.
- Skill: Factors & multiples

**Q16.** B - 13
- 12, 14, 15 are composite. 13 is prime.
- Skill: Primes

**Q17.** B - 5/10
- Total = 3+5+2=10. Blue = 5/10.
- Skill: Fractions

**Q18.** A - 25
- n = 42 − 17 = 25.
- Skill: Algebra thinking

**Q19.** B - 25
- Rule: start at 4, add 3 each time. 8th term = 4 + 7×3 = 4+21 = 25.
- Skill: Patterns

**Q20.** C - 2 500 mL
- 1 L = 1 000 mL, so 2.5 L = 2 500 mL.
- Skill: Measurement

**Q21.** C - 37.5%
- 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5%.
- Skill: Percentages

**Q22.** C - Right
- A 90° angle is a right angle.
- Skill: Angles

**Q23.** B - $5.00
- 1.25 × 4 = 5.00.
- Skill: Money

**Q24.** A - $7.20
- 20.00 − 12.80 = 7.20.
- Skill: Money

**Q25.** C - metres
- A classroom is typically several metres long — metres are the sensible unit.
- Skill: Measurement

**Q26.** B - 8
- (6+8+10) ÷ 3 = 24 ÷ 3 = 8.
- Skill: Statistics

**Q27.** C - 5
- 2 triangular ends + 3 rectangular sides = 5 faces.
- Skill: 3D shapes

**Q28.** A - Pad 8
- Each full move: +3 −1 = +2. After 4 moves: 4×2 = 8.
- Skill: Multi-step reasoning
